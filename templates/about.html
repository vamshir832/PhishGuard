About the PhishGuard project

PhishGuard is a local machine-learning application designed to help users evaluate the risk of a submitted website URL. It combines traditional feature engineering with a trained classifier to detect patterns commonly associated with phishing.

This project was built for educational and research use in computer networks and cybersecurity coursework. It emphasizes safe, offline analysis, model transparency, and a clear explanation of how URL characteristics contribute to the final risk prediction.
